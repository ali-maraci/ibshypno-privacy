Privacy Policy — IBS Hypno

Publisher: chemonitor
Contact: chemonitor@gmail.com
Effective date: 2026-11-11
App: IBS Hypno (Android)

Summary

IBS Hypno keeps everything you enter on your device. The only thing the app sends over the internet is a request for the session audio you choose to play, which streams over HTTPS from a server run by Cloudflare. We do not collect your answers, symptoms or check-ins, and there is no account.

What data the app stores on your device

The app uses Android's local DataStore to remember your preferences and progress on your device only. This includes:

  • Reminder settings (enabled/disabled, time of day, frequency)
  • Onboarding answers (age range, symptom history, program choices)
  • Program progress (current week, sessions completed, streak count)
  • A user-entered display name (optional)
  • A user-entered GP (General Practitioner) name (optional; stored locally for your own reference — it is not sent to any GP or to any server)
  • Pre-session and post-session self-reported symptom ratings
  • Language preference

What we do NOT collect

  • No account. No sign-up.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs.
  • No account, answers, symptoms or check-ins are ever sent to us or to anyone else. The only network requests the app makes are those that stream session audio (see “Session audio streaming” below).
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.
  • No health data shared with Google Health Connect or any other platform.

Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications (Android 13+): used only to show reminder notifications you schedule yourself. Decline-able; the app still works.
  • Internet: used only to stream session audio.
  • Wake lock: used only to keep audio loading while the screen is off.
  • Receive boot completed: used only to re-register your reminder alarms after the device reboots.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. Session audio is streamed through Cloudflare (Workers and R2, with storage in the EU), which is the only processor involved. See “Session audio streaming” below.

Session audio streaming

Session audio streams over HTTPS from a Cloudflare Worker in front of storage in the EU. Each request carries your device's IP address, the name of the recording, the time of the request, an app key (the same for every install) and a User-Agent of the form IBSHypno/<version>. Our server stores and logs nothing. Cloudflare keeps short-lived rate-limit counters keyed by IP address. No account, answers, symptoms or check-ins are sent.

Data retention and deletion

All data is stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage from Android Settings → Apps → IBS Hypno → Storage → Clear data.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. It is intended for adults managing IBS symptoms.

Medical disclaimer

IBS Hypno is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice. If you have new or worsening symptoms (such as unexplained weight loss, bleeding, severe pain, or fever), stop using the app and contact your GP or emergency services.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins collecting any data (for example, if analytics are added in a future release), this policy will be updated before that release, and you will be notified in-app on the next launch.
